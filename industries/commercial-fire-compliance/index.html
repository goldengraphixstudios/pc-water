/ Compliance Challenges

What Commercial Property Owners Face

AS2304 Fire Storage Compliance

Fire water tanks must be designed, installed, and documented to AS2304 — the standard specifically for water storage in fire protection systems.

AS1851 Annual Inspection

Commercial fire water tanks require annual inspection and maintenance under AS1851. Failure to maintain current inspection records creates insurance and regulatory risk.

Pump System Integration

Fire water storage tanks must be correctly integrated with fire pump systems — flow rates, connections, and detection must all meet the fire engineer's specification.

Ongoing Compliance Risk

Even a compliant tank at installation can fall out of compliance over time through corrosion, fitting deterioration, or capacity issues — requiring ongoing inspection and maintenance.
